Neural World Model / Imagination Engine

A CNN world model learns "what happens if I do this" from a bouncing ball. At every step it imagines 22 candidate futures 14 steps ahead, then acts on the one that scores best — before anything happens for real.

How it decides: the encoder compresses the current frame into a latent vector. A transition network rolls that latent forward under many random action sequences — pure imagination, no real environment steps. Each imagined future is scored by how close it keeps the ball to the target height near the top of the frame. The first action of the highest-scoring plan is the one actually taken.
Environment: 48×48 bouncing-ball physics sandbox  ·  Model: CNN encoder/decoder + residual latent transition (PyTorch, CUDA)  ·  Planning: random-shooting MPC over —-step imagined rollouts
